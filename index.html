The beacon has one last signal.

You wake on cool tiles in a coastal observation house you don't remember entering. Salt hangs in the air. Somewhere a brass speaker crackles and a calm voice repeats: "The beacon has one last signal. Send it before the tide returns."

Turn with the arrows. Tap anything that looks interesting to look closer. Pick up items, select one, then tap where to use it. Photograph clues with the camera; they go in your notebook.

      How to play

      Use the arrows at the sides of the view (or the arrow keys) to turn around a room. Tap objects to look closer, and Back (or Esc) to step back. Tap an item in your inventory to select it, then tap where to use it. On a computer you can also drag an item onto the scene. Tap a selected item, then another item, to try combining them. The camera saves the current view to your notebook, where you can draw on it. Hints come in three steps per puzzle: a nudge, a connection, then the answer. Your progress saves automatically.

      On iPhone, Safari doesn't allow true full screen for web pages. Turning your phone sideways gives the biggest view.
